HARD RULES — a first draft breaks both of these by default, so check them explicitly:

1. **NO STAR STORIES.** Never narrate an anecdote ("when X threatened Y, I did Z, so W").
   Stories are interview material; on paper they eat the whole page and read as padding.
   State the achievement as a short, quantified claim instead and let them ask about it.
   The letter's job is to earn the conversation, not to have it.
2. **KEEP IT SHORT — ~3 short paragraphs, well under one page.** Five paragraphs is too
   long. If a sentence needs a subordinate clause to set up context, cut it.
   Ballpark: ~1,500–1,800 characters of body text, not 3,500.
