# Prospects Shortlist — Sourcing Pipeline

The stage **before** an application exists: sweep what is actually open, score it against
`profile/company-fit.md`, and decide what is worth a tailored CV. Nothing here has a folder
yet — a prospect graduates to `applications/<company>-<role>/` via the `new-application`
skill, which is where the Golden Rule #12 gate and the comp/capability checks run.

> ### 🗄️ Before adding OR reviving a prospect, check BOTH indexes
> [`_index.md`](_index.md) (open) **and** [`_archive/_index.md`](_archive/_index.md) (closed —
> **where every past rejection lives**, Golden Rule #14).
>
> **Why this warning is at the top of the file:** a shortlist is rebuilt from job boards and
> inbox sweeps, and those sources have no memory. Left unchecked it will cheerfully re-list a
> company that already said no — and because sourcing happens *before* `new-application` runs,
> **Golden Rule #12 has not fired yet.** That is the whole point of the ⛔ table below: it moves
> the repeat-applicant check **one stage earlier**, to the moment a name is first written down,
> instead of catching it after the research is already spent.

**Method: market-FIRST.** Sweep what is *actually open now* at the target level and location,
**then** score each against values. Do **not** pick admired companies and hunt for a role —
that inverts the funnel and fills the list with wishes instead of options.

**Verification depth:** confirm the exact live posting, level and work mode **on the company's
own careers page** before building any CV. Job boards go stale and mis-state level constantly.
Mark anything unconfirmed with ⚠️.

**Constraints** *(fill from `profile/preferences.md` + `profile/company-fit.md`)*:
location / relocation · acceptable work modes · target level(s) · comp floor · any
target-role filter the user recorded. Source on leadership, culture and domain — **not** on
programming language, unless the user's own preferences say otherwise.

**Legend** — Fit: 🟢 strong · 🟡 good (vet) · 🟠 verify. Define domain codes for the user's own
market as you go (e.g. FT=fintech/payments, EC=e-commerce, MP=marketplace, ID=identity).

## ⛔ Recently rejected / exhausted — do NOT re-source

**This table is the highest-value part of the file.** It fires at *sourcing* time, one stage
earlier than Golden Rule #12.

⚠️ **It only works if closed applications are written into it**, and the failure is silent: a
company missing from this table is **indistinguishable from a company that was never
considered**, so the next sweep re-lists it as a fresh prospect and the research is spent again
before anything catches it. **Add the row as part of the §2.5 close-out**, while the archive
edit is already open — a table that depends on someone noticing later is a table that is
already out of date.

Not every row is a permanent no. Mark the distinction explicitly, because it decides whether
the name may ever be revived:
- **do-not-source** — a **structural** reason (level, tenure, domain depth) that re-tailoring
  cannot change.
- **pursue-with-eyes-open** — a **fixable positioning** reason, or a culture fit strong enough
  to justify another attempt. Say what would have to be different.
